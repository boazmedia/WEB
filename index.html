Repair tips

Before you bring it in

Simple things that protect your device and your data.

Screens

Cracked screen?

Cover the cracks with clear tape to avoid cuts and keep dust out, and back up your data. Repair early before the touch layer fails.

Read more
Water damage

Phone got wet?

Switch it off, don't charge it, and remove the case and SIM. Bring it in fast: corrosion spreads quickly. Rice doesn't help.

Read more
Batteries

Swollen battery

If the back lifts or the screen bulges, stop charging and using it. Don't press or puncture it. Get it replaced right away.

Read more
Locks

Locked out?

Bring proof you own the device and any account details you remember. We only unlock devices that belong to the customer.
