About this service

When a phone will not power on, the fix is often on the motherboard. Using microscopes and precision soldering stations, we repair individual components instead of replacing the whole device.

What we fix

Phone dead or not powering on
IC replacement
Charge port and charging IC faults
Baseband and network faults
Water-damage cleanup and component repair
No display or backlight at board level

What to bring

  • Your device, even if it looks dead
  • Details of what happened (drop, water, power surge)
  • Your charger and original cable if you have them
